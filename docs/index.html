Methodology

How It Works

Built as a Master's thesis in Business Analytics at Universidad del Rosario, this tool applies unsupervised machine learning to identify candidate profile patterns.

01

Extract Features

Four structural features are extracted from your CV: total text length, number of recognized sections, presence of a personal website, and presence of a training/courses section.

02

Profile Matching

Features are scaled and compared against 3 candidate profiles discovered through K-Means clustering of 600+ CVs collected from the Manatal recruitment platform.

03

Receive Feedback

You receive a profile assignment with a success rate indicator and specific, actionable recommendations to improve your CV's effectiveness in recruitment processes.

Strong Profile

Website + Training Section present. Highest success rate in recruitment.

Standard Profile

Training section present, no website link. Medium success rate.

Needs Improvement

Short CV, missing key sections. Lowest success rate — high improvement potential.
